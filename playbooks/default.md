# Default Legal Review Playbook

Focus on practical contract risk, clear drafting, and business-friendly explanations.

## Review checklist

- Identify ambiguous obligations, dates, scope, fees, acceptance criteria, and renewal mechanics.
- Flag one-sided indemnity, liability, IP ownership, confidentiality, termination, governing law, and dispute resolution terms.
- Suggest concise replacement wording when a clause can be improved.
- Separate legal risk from negotiation/business risk.
- Assign severity as High, Medium, Low, or Note.
